# International Expansion Potential (BONUS slide) - Family License

Date: 2026-11-14. Primary market: USA. Product: couple-synced gamified parenting skills subscription (~$24.99/4 wks, $149/yr household).

**Evidence legend:** [S] primary/official source, [SEC] secondary, [E] our estimate. "(v)" = figure surfaced in this session's web search and quoted from the result; "(u)" = from analyst background knowledge, NOT re-verified this session (check the named source before putting on a slide). Web search in this session returned thin country-level data (Sensor Tower per-capita spend is paywalled), so the sizing relies on transparent [E] formulas.

## 1. Headline

- Raw addressable revenue if every market were penetrated like the US: **US + Anglosphere = ~1.31x US; + DE/NL/Nordics = ~1.55x US** [E].
- Risk-adjusted (lower brand awareness, localisation friction, free state alternatives): **~1.23x after Anglosphere (Y2-3), ~1.35x after continental Europe (Y4)** [E].
- Order: Canada + UK + Australia (Y2) -> Ireland + NZ (Y2-3, near-free) -> Germany -> Netherlands -> Nordics (Y3-4, localised).
- Caveat: the "1.4-1.5x" rule of thumb is reachable only at full penetration; our sized figure is lower because UK/CA/AU together are only ~0.37x US in couples and net ARPU is ~0.75-0.85x after VAT/GST.

## 3. Expansion sizing table

Method [E]: couples = births x 5.4 (section 2.1). Net ARPU factor = list-price parity (about 0.9-1.0 of US) x (1 - VAT/GST drag, since app-store prices are VAT-inclusive) x WTP discount for free state alternatives. CAC factor = (CPM ratio ~0.55-0.75) / (conversion discount 0.7-0.8 for no brand/proof). Net revenue index = (couples / 19.5M) x ARPU. Risk factor = share of that index realistically reachable by Y4 (localisation, awareness).

| US + Anglosphere (UK, CA, AU, IE, NZ) | **1.31x** | **1.23x** |

Sensitivity: if non-US ARPU were 0.95 (USD price parity, no VAT drag) and penetration equalled the US, Anglosphere reaches ~1.40x; the "1.4-1.5x" upper bound requires roughly that.

### Spanish-speaking segment [E]

- US Hispanic/Latino ~19-20% of the US population (u) census.gov, so the same funnel in Spanish extends the US market without new compliance; incremental reachable couples with kids ~ +10-15% of US total, ARPU ~0.6-0.7x, lower CPMs. Treat as a Y3 localisation test (Spanish UI + content).
- Latin America: large population but ARPU ~0.2-0.3x US and payment friction (cash vouchers/OXXO, PIX in Brazil); not recommended before Y4.

## 4. Recommended order and timing

| Wave | Markets | Timing | Why | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Canada, UK, Australia | Year 2 (H1 soft launch Canada, H2 UK/AU) | Same language, same social platforms/funnels, 0.5-0.75x CPM, ~37% of US couples combined; Canada is closest culturally | Spelling (colour), units (kg/cm/Celsius, UK stones), local currency pricing, VAT/GST-inclusive pricing, term-name swaps, French for Quebec (or exclude Quebec initially), UK GDPR/AADC, AU Privacy Act |
| 2 | Ireland, New Zealand | Year 2-3 | Near-zero marginal localisation; small | Reuse UK/AU assets, EUR/NZD pricing; Irish GDPR |
| 3 | Germany | Year 3 (after English-wave proof) | Biggest continental pool (3.7M couples), but needs German product + app-store pricing + PayPal/SEPA for web checkout | Full translation and cultural adaptation (not literal), Datenschutz, cancel-button, Impressum, 14-day withdrawal, Elternzeit-based pacing |
| 4 | Netherlands | Year 3-4 | Highest English proficiency; English-first/Dutch-light possible | iDEAL on web paywall, GDPR, kraamzorg-aware onboarding |
| 5 | Nordics (SE, DK, NO) | Year 4 | High income, high English, but strong free state programmes and egalitarian leave; lowest incremental pain on language but WTP uncertain | Local-language for mass market; GDPR (Norway via EEA); lean on partner-alignment not "how to share leave" |
| Opt. | US Spanish | Year 3 test | No new compliance | ES UI/content |

### What to localise (checklist)

1. Language: spelling variants (en-GB/AU/CA), idioms, "Mum/Mom", "nappy/diaper", "pram/stroller", "toddler groups".
2. Units and formats: metric/imperial, dates DD/MM, currency and tax-inclusive price display (EU/UK/AU consumer law requires VAT-inclusive prices).
3. Health-system references: replace US pediatrician/WIC/insurance references with NHS/GP/health visitor (UK), Medicare/maternal-child health nurse (AU), Hebamme/U-Untersuchungen (DE), consultatiebureau (NL), BVC (SE).
4. School system names: Reception/Year 1 (UK), Prep/Foundation (AU), Kindergarten (CA), Kita/Grundschule (DE), Basisschool groep 1 (NL), forskola (SE).
5. Legal copy: no therapy/medical/treatment claims; disclaimers; no outcome guarantees; auto-renew and cancellation compliance; consent banners; children's-data handling and parental consent; DPIA.
6. Payment: app stores first (cards/Apple Pay), web checkout via Stripe with iDEAL, SEPA, PayPal, Klarna, Bancontact, Swish for EU.
7. Social proof: local testimonials, local influencers, and midwife/NCT/health-visitor partnerships.
8. Pricing: psychological prices (GBP 19.99, AUD 34.99, EUR 22.99) with parity ~0.9x of USD and annual household plan; test, do not copy.

## 5. Risks and unknowns

- Per-capita app spend not obtained (paywalled Sensor Tower table); UK/DE are known top West-EU consumer markets but our ARPU factors are judgments.
- Meta CPM benchmarks are vendor-reported and vary 0.5-0.75x of US; lower CPM does not guarantee lower CAC.
- Births and families figures marked (u) should be refreshed from the official statistics offices before the final deck.
- Free government parenting resources (NHS, Raising Children Network, Plunket) are the largest threat to WTP in wave 1/2.
