## 2. Failures and cautionary tales
- **coParenter** is alive but stuck at 3.1★. AI plus human mediation has not scaled.
- **Free co-parenting tiers have disappeared** (AppClose Jan 2026, TalkingParents Mar 2026). Conflict products are expensive to serve, and the person paying is in a fight.
- **BabyGPT pulled from the US App Store.** Low-quality AI parenting wrappers are fragile.
- **Lasting** went quiet after Talkspace acquired it. Couples apps churn once the crisis passes.
- **Duolingo lesson:** engagement does not automatically turn into paid subscribers. Monetization has to be designed deliberately.

## 3. The 8 gaps in the market
1. **Content library, no habit loop.** Good Inside, Gottman and Lamaze are things you *watch*, not things you *practice*.
2. **One-partner usage.** Most products are used by one partner (usually mom). Couple-synced progress is rare, and pricing is often per person.
3. **Mom-centric.** Dads are an afterthought. Products aimed at men only started appearing in 2026.
4. **Crisis-driven churn.** Bought during a fight or a sleepless night, abandoned after.
5. **Thin AI wrappers vs. evidence-based programs.** Good UX has no evidence behind it; strong evidence (Triple P, Family Foundations, Gottman) comes with bad UX.
6. **Expensive passive content** ($279/yr Good Inside, $29.99/mo Lasting Parenting).
7. **No continuum.** Pregnancy, newborn, toddler, couple: four separate apps with no shared data.
8. **Kids' gamification gamifies the child** (Joon, Greenlight). Nobody gamifies **the parent's skill growth** with real practice.

## 4. Where we differentiate
| Gap | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Library, no practice | **Practice-first**: daily 5-minute "reps" with real scenarios, AI role-play rehearsal ("practice the conversation before you have it"), and a small real-life mission with the kid every day |
| One-partner usage | **Two seats, one plan.** Shared streak, a "team" level, partner nudges, a joint certificate |
| Mom-centric | Role-neutral by design. A **dad-first acquisition angle** is an underserved, cheap-to-reach audience |
| Crisis churn | A life-stage **continuum** (expecting → 0–1 → toddler → school-age), with each stage unlocking the next "license level" |
| Thin AI | AI as a **practice partner and debrief coach**, not an oracle. Content is built on the Gottman / PREP / Family Foundations / Triple P principles |
| Expensive | **One household plan covers both partners**: $24.99 per 4 weeks or $149/yr (≈$12.40/mo), vs Good Inside at ~$23–28/mo *per person* and couples therapy at $100–300/session |
| Kids gamified, not parents | **Parent skill tree + "Family License" levels.** Kid-facing elements are optional and non-transactional |
