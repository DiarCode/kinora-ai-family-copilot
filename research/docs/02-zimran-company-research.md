## TL;DR
- **What they are:** a Kazakhstan-born (Almaty), **bootstrapped consumer-subscription "lifestyle EdTech" studio**. They build **28-day-challenge micro-learning products** and sell them to English-speaking consumers (US first) through **paid social → web quiz funnel → trial → recurring subscription**.
- **Live brands:** **Coursiv** (AI-tools skills) and **Finelo** (trading and investing education). The original brand was Prosperi Academy. The company says it runs "4 product niches"; only 2 are named publicly.
- **Money:** about **$2M MRR, about $20M/year revenue** *(self-reported, 2023 podcast, repeated on LinkedIn)*. Today it is probably higher. Headcount is 225–300+. **No external funding** found.
- **Mindset they reward:** "be extra". Values: Extra Obsessed, Extra Curiosity, Extra Ambition, Extra GTD (getting things done), Extra Business Sense. They want shipped working products, monetization and LTV thinking.
- **Gap we can fill:** they have **no parenting, family or relationships product**. The format (28-day challenge, daily micro-lessons, streaks, AI personalization, web funnel) fits our idea one-to-one.

## 3. Business model: how they make money

```
Paid social video ad (Meta / TikTok / YouTube)
   → Web quiz funnel ("What's your trading style?", 10–30 questions, personalization)
   → Email capture → "Your personal plan is ready"
   → Web paywall (Stripe/PayPal, avoids the 15–30% App Store fee)
   → Intro trial: ~$6.93 / 1 week, or ~$19.99 first 4 weeks
   → Auto-renew: ~$29.99–39.99 / 4 weeks  (≈ $360–480/yr if retained)
   → Upsells: one-time packs $9.99–79.99 ("Unlimited AI tools", guides)
   → App download (iOS) for daily usage, logged in with the web-purchased account
```

- **Who pays:** English-speaking adults, **US first**, then Canada, the EU, Australia/NZ and LatAm. No public demographic split is available. By category norms, the self-improvement quiz-funnel buyer is typically **25–55** and skews female for wellness and male for trading.
- **Revenue engine:** **web-to-app subscription**. Revenue = (ad spend ÷ CAC) × LTV. The core skills are **creative production for ads, funnel CRO and price testing**.
- **Category risk:** auto-renewing subscriptions sold through quiz funnels draw scrutiny from consumers and regulators across the whole industry. **Pitch lesson:** show **transparent billing** (clear renewal price, one-click cancel) and **no inflated outcome claims**. It shows the jury we understand the category's main risk.

> On "IRR": no internal rate of return is published, and it isn't really a startup metric. For a bootstrapped, ad-funded subscription business the right lens is **ROAS / payback period** (how many days until an ad dollar returns), plus **LTV/CAC**. Category norms for profitable web-funnel players: **D0 ROAS 40–70%, payback within 30–90 days, LTV/CAC ≥ 3.** We use these in our own model (see the business model doc).

**Scale sanity check:** at about $20M/year with ~100K paying users, ARPPU is roughly **$200/year**. That fits a $30–40/month plan with typical 4–6 month average lifetimes.

## 6. Fit: integration vs standalone (summary; full analysis in the idea review)

| | **Inside Zimran (a new "niche")** | **Standalone startup** |
|---|---|---|
| Why it fits | Same playbook: 28-day challenge, micro-lessons, streaks, AI, web paywall. Zimran has no family vertical yet. Their media-buying machine and funnel know-how cut CAC | We own the brand and equity. Can raise from family-tech/edtech VCs. B2B2C (employers, insurers, hospitals) is a later option |
| Risks | Parents and couples are trust-sensitive buyers, so the product needs its own brand and very transparent billing. Brand dilution | No paid-acquisition engine. CAC is our biggest risk. Small team |
| Best pitch framing | "**Zimran's 3rd/4th niche: Family Skills.** Same machine, a new $1B+ audience, higher LTV because parenting lasts 18 years" | "A venture-scale family-skills company. Zimran is the ideal operator/partner" |

**Recommendation for the pitch:** build and present it as a **standalone product that is "Zimran-native"**. Use their exact funnel mechanics, and close with "this plugs into Zimran's machine as the next niche." The jury *is* Zimran, so show them we understand their business.
