**Takeaway:** a household plan at **~$150/year** costs **less than one couples-therapy session** and about **half of Good Inside**.

## 3. TAM / SAM / SOM

| Layer | Logic | People / households | $ / yr |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TAM** | Partnered parents 25–44 (~62.7M parents × ~70% aged 25–44 [E] × 73% partnered [E] ≈ **32M**) + **2.6M** partnered expecting parents per year. Price ~$100–150/household | **~34.6M people ≈ 17M households** | **~$2.6–3.5B** |
| **SAM** | Same, filtered by "overwhelmed most days" (48%) + all expecting couples | **~18M people ≈ 9M households** | **~$1.1–1.35B** (matches the ~$1B NA parenting-app + education market) |
| **SOM (3 years)** | 0.2–0.5% of SAM households. Benchmark: Good Inside reached 100K+ subscribers in ~5 years | **18K–45K paying households** | **$2.7–6.8M ARR** |
| **$100M ARR long-term** | ~460K paying households × ~$219 net/yr | **≈ 1.4% of TAM households, 5% of SAM** | Expansion: kids 6–12, co-parents after separation, UK/CA/AU English markets, Spanish-speaking US, gifting |

The $100M ARR path is **plausible but not trivial**. It needs ~4–5× Good Inside's subscriber base, which is why the **couple-invite and gifting loops** (lower CAC) and **life-stage continuity** (longer LTV) matter.

## 4. Sharpest persona (the "one user, one pain")

**"Jess & Mike", ages 29–36, first child aged 0–3 (or expecting), both working.**
- They read the parenting Reels and bought a book, then didn't finish it. ~42% look for advice online, and 58% feel overwhelmed by it.
- Since the baby they fight more, mostly about chores, sleep, screen time and "you never help". They're part of the 67% whose satisfaction dropped.
- She does most of the "parenting research". **He wants to be a good dad but doesn't know where to start**, and no product is built for him.
- They'd never go to couples therapy ("we're not *that* bad", "$200/hour"). They would spend $10–25/month on "becoming a better team".
- **The pain:** *"We love our kid, but we're turning into roommates who argue about logistics, and we're scared our kid feels it."*
