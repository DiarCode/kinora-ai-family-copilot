Who else is out there, and where Hearth fits

One line: Good Inside = content for mom · Paired = a couple without kids · Joon = a game for the kid · Kinedu = activities for the baby · Hearth = daily practice for the parent team, from pregnancy to age 12.

The 4 closest rivals

Parenting education · for mom

Good Inside

~$279/yr or $28/mo (per person)

Dr. Becky Kennedy’s library of videos, “scripts” for hard moments, Q&A, community.

  • + ~$34M revenue (2025), 100K+ members: proof parents pay
  • + Trusted face, practical scripts
  • − Content you watch, not practice
  • − Mom-centric, 1 user, no partner sync
  • − Expensive, depends on one founder
Couples app · no kids

Paired

~$79.99/yr per couple

Daily questions for both partners, quizzes, expert audio tips.

  • + Real couple sync, daily habit
  • + 4–8M downloads, $4.6M raised
  • − “Maintenance, not repair”
  • − No children or parenting angle
  • − Churn after the novelty fades
Gamified chores · for the kid

Joon

~$12.99/mo or $89.99/yr

Kids complete real routines as “quests” in a pet game; parents approve.

  • + Kids genuinely engage
  • + Helps daily routines
  • − Points for normal behavior can erode a child’s own motivation
  • − Doesn’t build parent skills
  • − Child accounts mean COPPA
Baby development · ages 0–3

Kinedu

7 days free → $79.99/yr

Daily play activities matched to the baby’s development stage + milestone tracker.

  • + Clear daily value, personalized
  • + Uses a Zimran-style web quiz funnel
  • − About the baby, not the parents or the couple
  • − Stops being useful around age 3
  • − Mom-centric
