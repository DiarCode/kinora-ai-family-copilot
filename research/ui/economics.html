Hearth: key metrics

A 5-year cohort model in 4 scenarios. Pricing: $9.99 for the first 4 weeks → $24.99 every 4 weeks per household (both partners), or $149/yr. All numbers come from model/financial_model.py; assumptions are estimates anchored to 2026 subscription-app benchmarks (RevenueCat, Adapty, FunnelFox).

ARR by year, all scenarios

Where the money goes (Y5)

AI + servers ≈ 5–10% of revenue. Ads ≈ 60% of all costs. The business is won on acquisition cost, not AI cost. Lessons are pre-written; only Rehearsal, Repair summaries and personalization call an LLM (~$0.45/household/mo), and infra (hosting, DB, email) adds ~$0.30–0.50.

5-year plan

Word of mouth is the biggest lever

Base scenario, same ad budget. Only the share of customers who arrive free changes (partner invites, mom-to-mom recommendations, shared license cards, gifts).

We plan with only 15–25% organic until real data proves more. A model that assumes virality gets discounted.

US market

$100M ARR ≈ 460K paying households ≈ 4–5% of the able-to-pay pool. International: + UK/CA/AU/IE/NZ ≈ 1.3×, + DE/NL/Nordics ≈ 1.5× (estimates).

Unit economics per paying household (24 months)

Blended CAC = paid CAC × (1 − organic share) + AI cost of free users who try the app but don't buy. LTV = 24-month profit after payment fees, refunds, AI, servers and support. LTV/CAC above 1 means ads pay back; 3+ is healthy.
