## The five-minute script

**1. Cover (0:00–0:20)**
"Parenting is a team sport — but nobody gives parents a shared operating system. Kinora is the AI copilot for the parent team: five minutes a day, both parents, one shared plan. We have a working product and we're raising a $2.5M seed."

**2. Problem (0:20–0:55)**
"Parents are running a two-person job with no shared operating system. Forty-eight percent say stress is completely overwhelming most days, versus 26% of other adults. Couples wait about six years before seeking help, and the help that exists costs $100–300 a session."

**3. Solution (0:55–1:35)**
"Kinora is one calm place for the parent team. Check in for ten seconds each, private by default. Talk in a structured session that hears both sides and ends with one agreement. Agree on one next step, saved to a shared family memory. Five minutes a day. This is coaching, not therapy; crisis language routes to 988, 911, and the DV hotline before coaching."

**4. Why Kinora wins (1:35–2:05)**
"The market sells content or crisis care. Kinora runs the relationship. Good Inside is a library; BetterHelp is a crisis; ChatGPT has no partner seat and no shared family memory. Kinora is the only two-person daily workflow — and the table shows it job by job."

**5. Market (2:05–2:30)**
"Bottom-up: 17 million partnered U.S. households with kids under 12 at $149 a year is a $2.5B TAM. The 8.2 million households where parents are overwhelmed most days are a $1.2B SAM. Our three-year SOM is 18,000–45,000 households, or $2.7–6.7 million ARR. Good Inside did $34 million with 100,000+ subscribers — parents pay."

**6. Business model (2:30–2:55)**
"One household price: $149 a year for both partners, less than one therapy session. CAC is $67, 24-month LTV is $155 at an 80% gross margin, LTV/CAC is 2.3×, and payback is seven months. AI and hosting are about 8% of revenue. AI is not the risk — acquisition and retention are the business."

**7. Path (2:55–3:20)**
"The base case is $29.4 million ARR in Year 5; the optimistic case, with top-quartile retention, is $106.9 million. The $100 million path needs 460,000 households at about $219 net a year — 5.6% of the overwhelmed SAM. The base needs about $9.5 million of cumulative capital; this seed funds the first $2.5 million."

**8. Go-to-market (3:20–3:45)**
"Every customer brings a second seat and a third family. One parent starts, the app invites the partner free, and the daily loop becomes the retention loop. The US has no parent committee, so we built one into the app. Blended monthly CAC is $52; paid social only scales while payback stays under 12 months."

**9. Proof and pilot (3:45–4:20)**
"The product is real: six modules, 73 unit tests, 29 end-to-end tests, live voice, and a working safety path. We are pre-revenue. The next milestone is a 1,000-family pilot measuring partner-invite conversion, 14-day both-partner activation, D30 retention, and willingness to pay. If activation is below 40%, we launch a solo-parent mode at $79 a year and keep team mode as the upsell."

**10. Team and ask (4:20–4:45)**

Every parent deserves a teammate."

## VC Q&A prep

**Why not just ChatGPT?**
ChatGPT is one person talking to a general assistant. It has no partner seat, no shared family memory, and no structured two-sided session. Kinora is a two-person workflow with a curriculum, a memory, and guardrails. The durable moat is the shared memory and the trust brand, not the model.

**Is this therapy?**
No. Kinora is coaching and skills education. It does not diagnose, treat, or provide psychotherapy. Crisis and abuse language routes to 988, 911, and the DV hotline, and a safety pause blocks coaching. We use "coaching, not therapy" on every surface, and we will get counsel review before launch. We do not claim HIPAA.

**Will dads use it?**
The framing is competence, not therapy. The second seat is free, the invite is framed around the kid, and couples groups keep dads better than dad-only groups. We are testing a Dad License message and a mom-first message in the pilot.

**How do you get both partners?**
One parent can start; the second seat is free. We measure both-partner activation in 14 days as a core metric. If the partner does not join, the product still works solo; the shared memory is simply smaller. This is the main risk, which is why the 1,000-family pilot exists and why the contingency is explicit: below 40% activation, launch a solo-parent mode at $79 a year.

**Is the market real?**
17M partnered households with kids under 12 at $149 = $2.5B TAM. The 48% overwhelmed filter gives 8.2M households and a $1.2B SAM. SOM is 18K–45K households, $2.7–6.7M ARR. The 48% filter and the $149 price are the assumptions to challenge; the math is on the market slide.

**LTV/CAC is only 2.3. Why is this venture-scale?**
2.3× is the annual anchor at an 80% gross margin: $67 CAC, $155 24-month LTV, seven-month payback. The monthly floor is 1.4×. The free partner seat, gifting, and referrals lower blended CAC; retention after year one is what moves the multiple. A two-person habit is the moat.

**Pre-revenue?**
Yes. The product is shipped; the company is pre-revenue. This round funds the first 1,000-family pilot and the retention evidence. We are not claiming product-market fit from a demo. The 24-month milestones are 6,000 paying households and ~$900K ARR.

**What drives retention?**
Two people hold each other accountable: a shared streak, a partner who sees your progress, a weekly team ritual, a shared family memory they would lose, and life-stage levels that give a reason to stay after the first crisis. The target is 45% D30 and 60% both-partner activation.

**What is the regulatory risk?**
Illinois WOPR, Nevada AB 406, and about 20 state laws across 16 states in 2026. We design into the self-help/education exception, never call it therapy, never diagnose, and route crises. We will get counsel review and sequence launches state by state. COPPA: no child accounts under 13, minimal child data, no ad-tech sharing. We do not claim HIPAA.

**Why you and this team?**
I designed, built, tested, and shipped the entire product: six modules, the engine, the safety path, the voice rooms, 73 unit tests, 29 e2e tests. I can ship. Danila owns growth, pricing, and the financial model. Daniyar owns product execution and customer satisfaction. We are hiring the clinical leadership this category requires.

**What could kill this?**
Both-partner adoption and willingness to pay. We measure both in the first 1,000-family pilot. If they fail, we launch solo-parent mode at $79 and keep the product lean. The regulatory patchwork is a third risk; we design into the education exception and get counsel review.

**What does the $2.5M buy?**
24 months: 6,000 paying households, ~$900K ARR, 45% D30, 60% both-partner activation, a clinical advisory board, an independent outcomes study, and three channel partnerships. Use of funds: 35% growth, 30% product and AI, 25% clinical and content, 10% operations.
