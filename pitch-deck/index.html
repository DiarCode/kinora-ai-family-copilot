What we can honestly say

On the model's 91% contribution basis, any one assumption a third worse than the market median still pays back (LTV/CAC ≥ 1.05). At the deck's 80% gross margin, the annual anchor is 2.3× and the monthly floor is 1.4×; the stress case is below 1, which is why the stop rule exists.

AI and hosting 4×: LTV/CAC 1.20. AI is not the risk.

All seven levers pessimistic: LTV/CAC 0.33 at 91% contribution (0.29 at 80% GM) — every paid customer loses money. Our stop rule caps the loss at ~$90K after a $50K ad test.

Not claimed: “profitable in the worst case.” The company needs funding while it grows because ads are paid before customers pay back.

The two must-hold levers are the product design: retention at median (two seats, shared memory, weekly ritual) and CAC at median (free partner seat, gifting, referrals).

Appendix · Safety & regulationA3

Trust is architecture, not a disclaimer.

Today

  • Coaching and skills education — explicitly not therapy.
  • Two-sided sessions; no verdicts, no one-sided validation.
  • Crisis language stops the session: 988, 911, DV hotline.
  • Minimal child data: first name, birth date, observations.
  • No child accounts under 13. No ad-tech sharing.
  • Private moods by default; shared only when chosen.
  • Safety screening runs before generation.

Before launch

  • One-click delete; short retention; data-flow map.
  • Counsel opinion on the coaching classification.
  • Clinical advisory board named; clinical lead hired.
  • Crisis false-negative testing and human escalation protocol.
  • COPPA parental-consent review; no child data in model training.

What we don’t claim

  • “Therapy,” “treatment,” “diagnosis,” “cure.”
  • “Clinically proven” or “science-backed outcomes.”
  • “HIPAA-compliant” — we are not a covered entity.
  • “Prevents divorce” or “saves marriages.”
  • “AI therapist” or “replaces a clinician.”
  • “Detects emotions” or mental states.
  • “COPPA-compliant” without full amended-rule compliance.

The regulatory map

  • Illinois WOPR Act (2025): only licensed professionals may provide therapy; AI may not directly interact in “therapeutic communication”; self-help/education exception; up to $10K/violation.
  • Nevada AB 406 (2025): AI may not provide professional mental/behavioral care; self-help exception; up to $15K/violation.
  • ~20 state laws across 16 states in 2026; more in 2027.
  • FTC 6(b) inquiry into companion chatbots; state AG warning.
  • COPPA amended rule full compliance April 22, 2026.
  • FTC Health Breach Notification Rule covers health apps.

Legal position: coaching and skills education under the self-help/education exception. Before launch: counsel opinion, clinical advisory board, COPPA review, crisis false-negative testing, and a human escalation protocol. State-by-state launch sequencing. This page is not legal advice.

Sources: Illinois Public Act 104-0054; Nevada AB 406; Baker Botts state-law survey (Oct 2026); FTC 6(b) resolution; Federal Register COPPA rule (2025); FTC HBNR (2024).
